Third-party components
======================

This source tree includes third-party components. Before redistribution, preserve
the notices required by each upstream project and verify the exact license terms
that apply to the binaries, libraries and network-delivered components actually
used by the product.

KeyAuth C++ SDK
  Upstream: https://github.com/KeyAuth/KeyAuth-CPP-Example
  Bundled under: src/project/third_party/keyauth/x64
  Preserve the upstream licence and notices included with the SDK and its

Dear ImGui and other bundled components
  Keep the license/notice information supplied with the corresponding upstream
  source or asset. Do not infer one license for every third-party file.

w3w-map
  Upstream reference: https://github.com/oyuh/w3w-map

cs2_webradar
